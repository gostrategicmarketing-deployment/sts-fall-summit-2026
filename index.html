How these numbers are built

Registrations and sales come from Hyros. Spend, link clicks and impressions come from Meta, which bills them.

Ad accounts and creatives

  • STSSchool of Traditional Skills Ad Account1246959949464564
  • TSATraditional Skills Academy3014083142121289

Both sit under one Hyros account, so the two are combined without double counting.

The split is deliberate. Hyros counts people: it tracks server-side and catches registrations and sales the pixel drops. Meta counts money: it is what actually bills the card. Hyros relays cost rather than measuring it, and relays it late, so taking spend from Meta is what keeps this page matching Ads Manager to the cent. Ad sets are matched by ID, never by campaign name, so no other campaign in either account can leak in.

Creative previews are pulled from the Meta Ads API too, so they keep working after Meta's own image links expire.

The tag filter

Leads, purchases and revenue count only people carrying !summit-2026: 17870 leads in total, of which 17056 are attributed to a Fall Summit ad and 814 arrived organic or direct.

Spend and link clicks cannot be tag-filtered by anyone, so those are the whole-campaign figures for the Fall Summit ad sets.

Meta will report fewer registrations than this page does. Its pixel only sees what it can attribute in the browser; Hyros matches server-side. Reading the same window on 2026-09-11 05:43 PDT, Meta counted 27,571 against Hyros’ 17,056. Neither is broken: they count different things, and the gap is the reason Hyros is here.

A sale counts only where the buyer touched a Fall Summit ad. If they reached the offer through one, the sale counts whatever click closed it, credited to that ad. A tagged sale with no ad touch anywhere is organic traffic, not the ads, and is left out of every figure here.

Definitions

  • Page conversion is leads divided by link clicks, as requested. It can exceed 100% on an individual ad when Hyros tracks a session Meta did not count as a link click.
  • Cost per lead is spend divided by tagged leads.
  • Purchases and revenue are sales from !summit-2026 leads who touched a Fall Summit ad, inside the campaign window. The closing click may be anything; the ad touch is what qualifies it. Organic sales carrying the tag are excluded.
  • Reg. to sale is purchases divided by registrations: the share of people who signed up and then bought. Page conversion measures the ad and the landing page; this measures what happens after.
  • ROAS is that revenue divided by spend. Break-even is 1.00x.

Refreshing this page

This copy is rebuilt on request, not on a timer. Someone with the dashboard open presses Refresh, which re-pulls Hyros and rebuilds this page a couple of minutes later.
